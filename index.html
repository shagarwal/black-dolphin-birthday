Black Dolphin Inn

A 14-room inn on the Indian River in New Smyrna Beach. Private dock, sunsets over the water, and the beach a few minutes away across the causeway. Dolphins and manatees pass by the riverfront.

Rooms are posh and comfortable, many with river views; some have jetted tubs or private decks. You pick the room when we book.
