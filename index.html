Two nights at Black Dolphin Inn

New Smyrna Beach, Florida

A riverfront inn on the Indian River. Fourteen rooms, a private dock, sunsets that go gold over the water. Pick any weekend you like, and we'll set the dates together.

The Inn

Black Dolphin sits on the Intracoastal Waterway at the south end of Riverside Drive. The building faces west over the Indian River, which means every evening the light goes long and amber across the water and through the windows.

There's a dock where you can sit with coffee in the morning or a drink in the evening and watch for dolphins. They come through the river regularly, along with the occasional manatee. The beach and Flagler Avenue are a few minutes across the causeway.

The Rooms

Fourteen rooms, each a little different. River views, good linens, thoughtful details. Some have jetted tubs; some have private decks over the water. Your exact room will depend on when you go. All of them are worth waking up in.

While you're there

Dolphin Discovery Cruise

The Marine Discovery Center runs a naturalist-led boat cruise on the Indian River: a 40-passenger pontoon that goes out in the afternoon and evening, when the light and the wildlife are best. Dolphins, manatees, wading birds, and whatever else the lagoon decides to show. Departures from the North Causeway, a few minutes from the inn. Weather permitting, and sightings are never promised, but the river is full of life.

Our suggestion for Saturday afternoon. Say the word and we'll line it up.

New Smyrna has a quiet, real-town feel. Canal Street has good restaurants and small galleries. Flagler Avenue runs straight to the beach, and the surf break there is one of the best on the Atlantic coast. If you want to be on the water yourselves, there are guided kayak eco-tours through the mangroves and backwater channels.

A weekend, roughly

Friday evening

Arrive. Check in. Walk the dock. Watch the sunset from the water.

Saturday

Late breakfast at the inn. Beach morning or a kayak tour. Afternoon: the dolphin cruise on the river. Dinner on Canal Street or Flagler.

Sunday

Sleep in. Coffee on the dock. One more look at the river. Head home slow.

This is a suggestion, not a schedule. Do whatever you want.
